Algebra cheat sheet

The five skills on one page.

1

Collect like terms (5C)

Same letters, same powers. Add or subtract the coefficients.
3x2y + 2xy2 − x2y = 2x2y + 2xy2
2

Multiply (5D)

Multiply the coefficients. Add the indices of matching pronumerals.
3a3b × −2a2b2 = −6a5b3
4

Divide (5E)

Divide the coefficients (use the HCF). Subtract the indices. Cancel.
30a3b2 ÷ −6ab2 = −5a2
5

Expand (5F)

Multiply every term inside the bracket by the term outside.
−7p(3q − 4) = −21pq + 28p
6

Factorise (5G)

Take out the HCF and divide every term by it. Check by expanding.
15a3bc + 9a2c = 3a2c(5ab + 3)

Which skill?

  • Same letters & powers, adding or subtracting → collect
  • Terms × terms → multiply (add indices)
  • Terms ÷ terms → divide (subtract indices)
  • Brackets with a term outside → expand
  • A sum with a common factor → factorise

Check your answer

  • Substitute a number into the start and the end. They should match.
  • Factorised? Expand it back.
  • Is every term in the brackets divided by the HCF?

Common mistakes

  • 5m2 + 2n + m2 ≠ 6m + 2n. Correct: 6m2 + 2n
  • 9x − x ≠ 9. Correct: 8x
  • 3x × 4x ≠ 12x. Correct: 12x2
  • (4gh)3 ≠ 12g3h3. Correct: 64g3h3
  • −4 × −5x ≠ −20x. Correct: 20x
  • 3x + 4x ≠ 7x2. Correct: 7x
  • 7xy2 × 8x3y2 ≠ 15x4y4. Correct: 56x4y4
  • 2x + x2 − 5x + 3x2 ≠ −3x3. Correct: 4x2 − 3x
  • x4 ÷ x4 ≠ 0. Correct: 1
  • a5 ÷ a3 ≠ a8. Correct: a2
  • 30a3b2 ÷ −6ab2 ≠ 5a2. Correct: −5a2
  • −(a + 2) ≠ −a + 2. Correct: −a − 2
  • 3(x + 4) ≠ 3x + 4. Correct: 3x + 12
  • −7p(3q − 4) ≠ −21pq − 28p. Correct: −21pq + 28p
  • a2 − 6a ≠ a(a + 6). Correct: a(a − 6)
  • −4a − 12 ≠ −4(a − 3). Correct: −4(a + 3)
  • 7m + 2n − 5m − 2n ≠ 2mn. Correct: 2m
  • 2(x + 3) ≠ 2x + 3. Correct: 2x + 6
